Activities & Timing
⌛
Main Activities (35 minutes)
Introduction (5 minutes)
The teacher explains the purpose of the practice assessment and reminds students of the expectations for silent test conditions. Students prepare their equipment and listen to instructions.
Independent Assessment (30 minutes)
Students independently complete Science Practice Test 1 – Paper 1 under silent examination conditions.
The assessment covers food chains, skeletons, particle models, medicine safety, energy transfers and scientific investigations.
Students are encouraged to read each question carefully, use appropriate scientific vocabulary and check their answers if time allows.
The teacher supervises without providing assistance with answers.


Introduction (5 minutes)
The teacher reminds students of the expectations for silent test conditions and explains that Paper 2 will assess their understanding of previously taught scientific concepts.
Students prepare their equipment and receive their assessment papers.
Independent Assessment (30 minutes)
Students independently complete Science Practice Test 1 – Paper 2 under silent examination conditions.
The assessment covers muscles and movement, animal classification, energy transfers, infectious diseases, solids and liquids, and fair-testing investigations.
Students apply their knowledge to written questions, diagrams, data tables and scientific explanations.
The teacher monitors students throughout the assessment without providing assistance with answers.


Introduction (5 minutes)
The teacher returns both assessment papers and explains the importance of reviewing mistakes as part of the learning process.
Students reflect briefly on which questions they found easy or challenging.
Whole-Class Review – Paper 1 (12 minutes)
The teacher guides students through the answers to Paper 1, focusing particularly on questions that require scientific explanations and interpretation of results.
Students discuss correct answers, identify common misconceptions and make corrections to their work.
Whole-Class Review – Paper 2 (13 minutes)
The class reviews the answers to Paper 2, focusing on scientific vocabulary, particle models, energy transfers and fair-testing investigations.
Students are encouraged to explain their reasoning and use evidence from the questions to support their answers.
The teacher provides additional explanations where necessary.
Plenary – Reflection (5 minutes)
Students reflect on their performance across both papers and identify one area of science they understand well and one area they need to improve.
The teacher uses the assessment results and student reflections to identify topics requiring further revision before the final assessment.
